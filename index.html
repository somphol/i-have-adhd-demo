The 10 rules being demonstrated

From SKILL.md, MIT licensed. Abridged here; the file is the source of truth.

  1. Lead with the next action.
  2. Number multi-step tasks, one bounded action each.
  3. End with one concrete next step.
  4. Suppress tangents.
  5. Restate state every turn.
  6. Specific time estimates, never "a bit".
  7. Make wins visible.
  8. Matter-of-fact errors: location, cause, fix.
  9. Cap lists to 5 items.
  10. No preamble, no recap, no closers.
